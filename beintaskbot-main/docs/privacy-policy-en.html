2. Data processed

Depending on the enabled features, the service processes user and company identifiers, names and contact details, employee memberships and permissions, customer messages and attachments, CRM deals and tasks, notification settings and technical logs required for operation and security. CRM authorization tokens are used to access the account authorized by its owner.

3. Sources and purposes

Data comes from users, their authorized CRM accounts and connected communication channels. It is processed to display conversations, manage deals and tasks, deliver notifications, implement configured approvals, troubleshoot errors and protect the service.

4. External services and AI

Enabled integrations may involve the CRM provider, Telegram, messaging providers, Linear, OpenAI and hosting infrastructure such as Railway. These providers process relevant data under their own terms and privacy policies. When an AI feature is requested, conversation content, selected attachments or audio may be sent to the configured AI provider to produce a draft, summary or transcription. Users should not submit data they are not authorized to process.

5. Access, storage and protection

The service uses company-specific memberships and permissions to restrict access, along with protected integration credentials and security checks. Retention depends on the feature, company settings, operational needs and applicable requirements. Disconnecting a CRM stops its authorized access but is not a request to erase data already stored in the service.
