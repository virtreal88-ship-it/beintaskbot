Customer conversations · Deals · Team tasks

A shared workspace for your sales and service team.

CRM Assistant brings connected customer conversations, deals and tasks into a browser-based workspace. Administrators manage employee access and workflows; employees see the work assigned to them.

Create your company account

What you can do

Customers and deals

Review customer conversations, track deal stages and manage tasks linked to deals. Messaging channels must already be connected in your CRM.

AI assistance

Prepare a reply draft or a conversation summary. Review drafts before sending; AI does not send customer messages automatically.

Employee access

Invite employees through Telegram and configure the pages and actions available to each member of your company.

Notifications and approvals

Configure employee notifications and completion approvals according to your company's workflow.

Before installation

  1. Have an active Kommo account and administrator access. Your Kommo subscription is separate from CRM Assistant.
  2. Open registration and sign in with your own Telegram account as the company owner. Do not provide your Kommo password to BeinSystems.
  3. On the first setup step, choose the Kommo integration. Select your Kommo account on the authorization page and review the permissions requested by the integration.
  4. Grant access only to the account you want to connect. You will return to CRM Assistant after authorization.

After installation

  1. Check the connection using the test-connection action on the setup screen. If it fails, contact the support team above with the error message.
  2. Continue the setup questions to describe your company and its workflow. Select the pipelines and stages relevant to your business, then review and finish setup.
  3. Open your workspace. In Settings, add employees, assign their access and send individual Telegram invitation links.
  4. Use Customers to review conversations, Deals to track work and Tasks to assign follow-up actions. Review AI reply drafts and use Send only when you are ready to send a message.
  5. To stop CRM access, revoke the integration in Kommo's integration settings. To reconnect, use the integration connection action in CRM Assistant and authorize the correct account again.
